Editor's Letter · May 2026

On the fragrance
that lives twice.

A perfume should be a quiet rebellion. Against the perfectly-packaged sameness of mass-market scents. Against the four-figure price tags of the houses we admire from a distance. Against the idea that luxury must, by definition, be unreachable.

Beyzaaan is a maison built on a single conviction: that the master perfumers of the East — the same noses who quietly supply some of the world's best-known houses — could just as easily compose for us. Without the markup. Without the marketing tax. Without the four ad agencies in between.

Every bottle is sealed in wax, dated, and labelled by hand from our atelier in Cape Town. The juice inside carries the same craft as the bottle on the counter at Liberty. What's missing is what wasn't there to begin with — the noise.

The Process

From the souk
to your shelf.

I Composition

Every Beyzaaan formulation begins in the perfumeries of the East, where master noses still work from hand-written formulas in cedar-shelved laboratories. We brief, they compose, we revise — sometimes for months. No algorithm. No trend-led briefs. Just olfactory architecture.

II Maceration

The juice rests, sometimes for weeks, in glass before bottling. This is where the alchemy lives — what arrives in a bottle after maceration is dimensionally different from what was first composed. The shortcut is to skip this. We do not.

III Atelier finish

Every bottle that leaves our Cape Town atelier is wax-sealed, dated, and labelled by hand. The label carries the formulation number, the batch month, and — if you've asked us to — your name in iron-black ink. A luxury bottle should not arrive feeling industrial.
