Our Story

"Luxury fragrance
shouldn't cost a month's salary."

Beyzaaan was born from one frustration: the perfumes we wanted — the bold ouds, the powdered florals, the spicy aquatics — were locked behind R3,000 price tags. So we went to the source. Master perfumers in the East, the same houses that supply some of the world's biggest names, compose for us. The bottle in your hand carries the same craft as the one on the Dior counter. Without the four-figure markup.
